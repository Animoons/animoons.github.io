gego onadinaakegon a'aw goon don't make snowmen

miinawaa again, also noomaya not too long back wiin s/he igo well, ah ingikendaan I know of it i'iw that onadinangid as the other shaped an image out of a'aw that one in motion goon snow.  mii so ezhi-nishkaadizid as more or less the other is angry a'aw that one in motion  mindimooyenh elder woman, gego don't you all.  o-niigwa'ok break someone apart wewiib hurry, quick a'aw. that one in motion.

aaniin how danaa tell of aaniish how then indinaa I say to her, him.  mewinzha long ago giiwenh so the story goes abinoojiiyag children onadinaawaad that they shape into the image of someone,  mii so iniw the other(s) goonan other snow(s).  mii so giiwenh as the story goes ezhi-gichi-gisinag as it is more or less very cold.  mii so awiiya someone ezhi-wiindigoowid that in such a way the other is in the state or condition of an ice giant.  miish so then igo well, ah gaa-igooyaang as someone used to say to us gego dont ondainaakegon don't you shape the image of someone from a'aw that one in motion goon. snow.

geyaabi still go well noongom now, currently mii so enindwaa that it is said to them abinoojiiyag children gego don't ji-mazinadinaasiigwaa so that they do not make hill patterns from iniw the other(s) goonan other snow(s).  mii so giiwenh supposedly ezhi-bazigwiid that in such a way s/he stands up from sitting a'aw that one in motion wiindigoowid. as s/he is the state of an ice giant.
